Track 02 — AI Risk Manager

Fraud doesn't happen in one transaction. It happens over a trajectory.

Most fraud detectors score a single transaction in isolation. This one watches how a customer's behavior drifts over time — from normal, to probing, to active abuse — and turns that trajectory into a risk score a merchant can actually audit, not just trust.

Why this approach

A single-row classifier can't see a customer escalating. A hidden Markov model can — it tracks hidden behavioral state across an entity's transaction sequence and never sees the fraud label while training, so its states stay honestly unsupervised.

The bar we hold ourselves to

Honest precision, recall, and false-positive cost on a held-out test set — measured against a rule-based baseline and logistic regression, not just against ourselves. Strictly defense-only: every synthetic-fraud component exists to stress-test the detector, never to design an attack.

System design

Five stages, one decision

A transaction stream becomes a bounded, explainable decision.

01
Behavioral features
6-hour windows per entity: transaction velocity, amount statistics, device and email diversity, night-activity rate, and how much a transaction's amount deviates from the entity's own history.
02
HMM trajectory model
A 3-state Gaussian HMM tags each window NORMAL, PROBING, or ACTIVE_ABUSE, then a trajectory score blends the current state with how the entity got there — a fresh transition into abuse weighs differently than abuse that's been sitting there for a while.
unsupervised
03
Bayesian evidence fusion
Combines the trajectory score with independent behavioral flags — shared devices, multiple emails, rapid transactions — into one calibrated probability, with a plain-English explanation of exactly which evidence fired.
explainable
04
Synthetic stress test
A WGAN-GP generator trained only on real fraud rows produces synthetic fraud sequences, validated against real fraud with an adversarial realism check, to confirm the detector generalizes past the examples it saw in training.
defense-only
05
Decision + audit trail
Every score resolves to allow, review, or block — with the evidence that led there logged alongside it, so a compliance reviewer can trace any decision back to its cause.
